## 2. Research Framework Design

### Direction A: China Electric Vehicle Industry Research

#### Research Dimensions
1. **Market Size and Growth**
   - 2020-2024 Market Size
   - Sales Data and Growth Rate
   - Segmented Markets (Sedan/SUV/MPV)

2. **Competitive Landscape Analysis**
   - Major Enterprise Market Share
   - BYD, Tesla, NIO, XPeng, etc.
   - New Forces vs Traditional Automakers

3. **Technology Development Trends**
   - Battery Technology (LFP/Ternary Lithium)
   - Intelligence (Autonomous Driving, Smart Cockpit)
   - Charging Infrastructure

4. **Policy Environment**
   - New Energy Vehicle Subsidy Policy
   - Dual Credit Policy
   - Regional Policies

5. **Consumer Profile**
   - Buyer Demographic Characteristics
   - Purchase Decision Factors
   - Brand Preferences

### Direction B: User Vehicle Purchase Preference Survey

#### Survey Objectives
Understand Chinese consumers' EV purchase preferences, decision factors, and purchasing behavior

#### Sample Design
| Parameter | Value | Description |
|------|-----|------|
| Sample Size | 500 people | Representative Sample |
| Persona Template | Multi-template Hybrid | First-tier White-collar, New Middle Class, Young First-time Buyers |
| Stratification Dimensions | City + Age + Income | Ensure Sample Diversity |

#### Survey Design (To Be Confirmed)

**Part 1: Basic Information**
1. What is your age range?
   - A. Under 25  B. 25-35  C. 35-45  D. Over 45

2. What type of city do you live in?
   - A. First-tier City  B. New First-tier City  C. Second-tier City  D. Third-tier and Below

3. What is your annual household income?
   - A. Under 100K  B. 100-200K  C. 200-500K  D. Over 500K

**Part 2: Purchase Preferences**
4. Are you considering purchasing an electric vehicle?
   - A. Already Purchased  B. Currently Considering  C. Future Possibility  D. Not Considering

5. When purchasing an EV, which factors matter most? (Multi-select)
   - A. Range  B. Charging Convenience  C. Price  D. Brand Reputation
   - E. Smart Features  F. Design  G. Safety  H. After-sales Service

6. What is your expected range?
   - A. Under 300km  B. 300-500km  C. 500-700km  D. Over 700km

7. What is your budget range?
   - A. Under 100K  B. 100-200K  C. 200-300K  D. 300-500K  E. Over 500K

8. Which brand/type do you prefer?
   - A. BYD  B. Tesla  C. NIO/XPeng/Li Auto
   - D. Traditional Automaker NEV  E. Other

**Part 3: Usage Scenarios**
9. What is your primary usage scenario?
   - A. Daily Commute  B. Family Travel  C. Business Use  D. Other

10. Do you have fixed charging conditions?
    - A. Home Charger  B. Company Charger  C. Public Charger  D. No Fixed
