# Writing Style Guidelines - Professional Consulting Standard

## Basic Principles

1. Follow McKinsey/BCG/Deloitte professional consulting report standards
2. Use structured analytical frameworks (PEST, Porter's Five Forces, SWOT, Value Chain, etc.)
3. Present data through tables, charts, and visualizations, not just text
4. Each chapter should have clear hierarchy: Overview → Detailed Analysis → Key Findings → Implications
5. Use professional business language, avoid academic or colloquial expressions

## Chapter Structure (Professional Standard)

Each chapter should follow this structure:
```
## Chapter Title

### Executive Overview
- 2-3 paragraphs summarizing the key points of this chapter
- High-level metrics and trends

### Detailed Analysis
#### 1. [Analysis Dimension 1]
- Data presentation with tables/charts
- Analysis and interpretation
- Implications

#### 2. [Analysis Dimension 2]
- Data presentation with tables/charts
- Analysis and interpretation
- Implications

#### 3. [Analysis Dimension 3]
...

### Key Findings
- Bullet points of critical insights
- Supporting data points

### Strategic Implications
- What this means for stakeholders
- Actionable recommendations
```

## Data Presentation Rules

### Tables (Required)
- Every chapter MUST include at least one data table
- Tables should have clear headers, units, and sources
- Use professional formatting: alternating row colors, proper alignment

### Charts (When Applicable)
- Market size trends → Line chart
- Market share → Pie chart or bar chart
- Comparison data → Bar chart
- Correlation data → Scatter plot

### Data Integration
- Don't just list numbers, explain what they mean
- Compare with industry benchmarks
- Show trends over time (3-5 years when possible)
- Highlight significant changes or anomalies

## Prohibited Expressions

### Academic/Research Style (Prohibited)
- "核心结论" (Core Conclusion)
- "论证与分析" (Argument and Analysis)
- "数据支撑" (Data Support)
- "置信度" (Confidence Level)
- "假设验证" (Hypothesis Verification)

### Colloquial Expressions (Prohibited)
- "Worth noting"
- "Coincidentally"
- "Interestingly"
- "Have to say"
- "Let's look at"
- "Imagine"
- "Did you know"
- "This means"
- "In other words"
- "Simply put"

### Source Annotations (Prohibited in Body)
- (Multiple institutions predict)
- (Data shows)
- (According to research)
- [Source: xxx]
- [Source 15]

## Correct Expression Style

### Market Analysis
- ✅ "The market reached 16.49 million units in 2025, with penetration rate hitting 47.9%."
- ✅ "BYD dominates with 41.9% market share, followed by Tesla at 6.4%."
- ❌ "核心结论：市场实现历史性突破。论证：数据显示..."

### Competitive Analysis
- ✅ "The competitive landscape shows clear tier separation: Tier 1 (BYD, Tesla) with >5% share, Tier 2 (Geely, Changan) with 3-5%, Tier 3 (NIO, XPeng, Li Auto) with 1-3%."
- ❌ "竞争格局分析：核心结论是市场高度集中。论证：数据显示..."

### Technology Trends
- ✅ "PHEV technology has become the key growth driver, with exports surging 230% YoY to 969,000 units."
- ❌ "技术趋势：核心结论是插混技术崛起。论证：数据表明..."

## Professional Terminology

Use consulting-standard terminology:
- "市场渗透率" instead of "普及率"
- "竞争格局" instead of "竞争情况"
- "产业链" instead of "供应链"
- "投资回报率" instead of "收益率"
- "战略定位" instead of "发展方向"
- "价值主张" instead of "核心优势"

## Chapter Length Guidelines

- Executive Summary: 1-2 pages
- Each Analysis Chapter: 3-5 pages
- Conclusion & Recommendations: 2-3 pages
- Total Report: 30-40 pages

## Visual Elements Required

1. **Cover Page**: Professional design with logo, title, date, confidentiality level
2. **Table of Contents**: 2-3 level hierarchy with page numbers
3. **Executive Summary**: Key findings with metrics
4. **Data Tables**: At least 2-3 per chapter
5. **Charts**: At least 1-2 per chapter (when data permits)
6. **Key Insights Boxes**: Highlighted critical findings
7. **Footer**: Page numbers, confidentiality marking
8. **Disclaimer**: Standard legal disclaimer at end
